BirdoVPN is a fast, private VPN built on WireGuard®, with post-quantum key exchange, a kill switch and a strict no-activity-logs policy.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No activity logs — we do not record what you browse, download or connect to
• Warrant canary published and updated on our website
• Anonymous accounts — sign in with a 24-digit ID instead of an email if you prefer
• Delete your account and its data at any time, right from the app

MODERN, FAST PROTOCOLS
• WireGuard® tunnelling for high throughput and quick reconnects
• Post-quantum key exchange layered on top of the tunnel, protecting today's traffic against tomorrow's quantum attacks
• Stealth mode for networks that block or throttle VPN traffic

STAY PROTECTED
• Kill switch — if the tunnel drops, traffic stops instead of leaking
• DNS leak protection with encrypted DNS (DNS-over-HTTPS)
• Multi-hop — route through two locations for an extra privacy layer
• Biometric app lock to keep the app itself private

MADE FOR EVERYDAY USE
• Split tunnelling — choose which apps use the VPN and which go direct
• Global server locations with live latency so you can pick the best exit
• Home-screen widget for one-tap connect
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics SDKs in the app

Get started free — create an account (email or anonymous ID) and connect. Premium tiers add more locations and features.

BirdoVPN uses the Android VpnService API. The persistent notification while connected is required by Android for VPN apps.
